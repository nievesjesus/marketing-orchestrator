# Brand check

A quick, **silent** review of every draft before you show it (SKILL.md section 7). The user sees something only when a point is off, as a short note. It's a friendly reminder, **not legal advice**.

## What to check

Use the brand card as the reference. No card yet? Check clarity, consistency and professionalism only (is the point clear in the first line, is the tone steady, any typos), and skip the brand-specific points.

**1. Voice fit**
- Does the text match the card's 3 traits ("we are")? Does any line drift into the "we are not"?
- Is the tone right for the network (LinkedIn more measured, Instagram more relaxed)?
- Is the language the one the card asks for, and is the same register kept from start to end?

**2. Banned words and style rules**
- Any word from "Words to avoid" or "We never" (topics, promises, competitors).
- The emoji policy (none / a few / which ones) and capitals and punctuation rules (no ALL CAPS, one exclamation mark at most).
- Names of products, people and places spelled the way the brand writes them.
- Hashtags: the brand's own ones are present, none are unrelated or engagement bait.

**3. Claims to double-check**

| Kind of claim | Example | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| Superlatives | "the best", "the only", "number one" | Ask what backs it up; otherwise soften ("one of our favorites") |
| Health or body promises | "cures", "lose 5 kg", "natural therapy" | Remove or turn into a neutral description; suggest checking local rules |
| Money promises | "double your income", "guaranteed savings" | Remove, or use only a real, confirmed example with its conditions |
| Guarantees | "100% satisfaction", "or your money back" | Only if the user confirms the real terms; write the terms |
| Comparisons | "better than <competitor>" | Avoid naming others; compare with "before" or "how it used to be" |
| Testimonials | a customer quote, "our clients say…" | Only a real quote the user received, with their ok to use it; never invented |
| Prices, discounts, dates, stock | "$12 until Friday" | Ask the user to confirm numbers and dates; never fill them in yourself |
| "Free", "no obligation", "limited" | "free tasting" | Only if real; limited means a real limit |
| Others' content | a copied phrase, a photo from the web | Use the user's own material or generated images |

**4. Images** (when the post has a graphic)
- Text on the picture is spelled right and fits (compose tools draw it exactly; read their `warnings`).
- No other brands' logos, no real identifiable people (unless the user's own photo of themselves), no minors, no known characters.
- The user has the rights to the photos (their own, or provided by them).

## What to do with the findings

- **Fix the small stuff yourself** (a banned word, a tone slip, an extra exclamation mark) and mention it in one line: "I changed X to Y to match your voice."
- **Ask about anything the user must back up** (claims, prices, dates, testimonials) in one short note.
- **Show nothing** if everything is fine.

Format of the note (in the user's language), at most 4 lines:

```
Double-check before posting:
- "<the phrase>": <why it may need backing up> → <suggested fix, or the question>
- <next point>
These are reminders, not legal advice; rules differ by country and network.
```

For regulated topics (health, finance, alcohol, children, contests and giveaways), add one line recommending the user confirm the local rules or ask a professional. Don't go deeper than that.
