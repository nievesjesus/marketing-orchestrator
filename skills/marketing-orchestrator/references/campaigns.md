# Campaign mode

A campaign is a short run of posts around **one goal** and a date range. You plan it in plain steps, show a short card, and only then draft. **Approving the plan never schedules anything**: posts are scheduled only after the approval card (SKILL.md section 9).

Ask in small batches, in the user's language, offering a recommended option for each question. Never invent facts, numbers, reviews or prices; if the user has no proof, plan without it.

## Step 1. Goal (pick one) and one number

| Goal (plain words) | What success looks like | A number the user can count |
|---|---|---|
| Be known | More new people see us | Followers gained, people reached (from Postiz → Analytics) |
| Get interest | People react and look closer | Comments, saves, profile visits, link clicks |
| Get messages or sales | People write, book or buy | Messages received, bookings, orders |
| Bring customers back | Past customers return | Returning customers, replies from past customers |
| Get recommendations | People talk about us | New reviews, tags, "a friend sent me" |

Ask for **one main number and a date** ("20 bookings by 30 November"). If they don't know what's realistic, don't invent a target: note today's number as the starting point and aim to beat it. If they track nothing, choose something countable (messages received, followers gained).

## Step 2. Audience and message

- **Audience in one line:** [who] in [place] who wants [outcome] but [obstacle]; they find us on [network] and care most about [priority].
- **Message order:** why should they care → what is it → why us → what to do. Write one core sentence and up to 3 supporting points.
- **Proof:** only what the user actually has (their own photos, real customer comments they received, years in business, numbers they gave). No proof yet? Use a promise they can keep, or skip the proof post and say why.

## Step 3. Networks and rhythm

- 2–3 networks, chosen by where the audience is, not by habit.
- Default **2–4 posts per week per network**, at least **24 hours apart** on the same network.
- Total posts = networks × posts per week × weeks. If that is more than the user can maintain (photos, replying to comments), cut the rhythm, not the quality.
- Campaigns longer than 4 weeks: detail the first 2 weeks, outline the rest.

## Step 4. Choose an arc

Each slot has a **role**. Day offsets are starting points to adapt (T = the key date: launch day, event day, or the last day of a promo).

| Arc | Roles in order |
|---|---|
| **Launch** (new product, shop, service) | Teaser (T-10) → second teaser (T-5) → announcement (T) → spotlight on one benefit (T+2) → proof (T+5) → offer with real terms (T+9) → last call (T+13) → thank-you |
| **Event** | Save the date (T-21) → why come (T-14) → behind the scenes or meet the people (T-10) → details and how to sign up (T-7) → reminder (T-2) → day-of (T) → thank-you and highlights (T+2) |
| **Weekly series** (same format, same day) | Opener that sets expectations → episode → episode → community question → episode → best-of or recap → ask what they want next |
| **Seasonal promo** | Heads-up (T-7) → offer reveal (T-5) → ideas or reasons to use it (T-3) → proof (T-2) → countdown reminder (T-1) → last day (T) → thank-you |
| **Re-engagement** (bring customers back) | What's new since they came → a reminder of the value → a story or proof → an easy reason to return (real offer or news) → last nudge → thank-you |

Proof, offers and terms must be real and confirmed by the user (dates, prices, limits). A "last call" with a fake deadline is not allowed.

## Step 5. Calendar

Work **backward from the key date**: what has to be seen by when? Then fill:

| Date | Network | Post idea | Role | Visual | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Tue 14 Oct | Instagram | Teaser: the new menu, only the colors | Teaser | own photo / generated / composed template | idea |

- **Visual:** own photo (Postiz upload widget), generated background (+ composed headline), or composed template (graphic with exact text, carousel). Keep one look across the campaign: same saved brand, 2–3 templates.
- **Status** moves through: idea → drafted → image ready → approved → scheduled.
- **Leave about 20% of the slots free** for reactions, news, or a post the user decides to add.
- Spread the posts: avoid two posts on one network within 24 hours, avoid stacking everything in one week.
- Batch by theme and make visuals in batches; each AI image needs the quote and ok first (SKILL.md section 5).

## Step 6. Success numbers, risks, next step

- **Success in plain words:** repeat the goal and the number, and when you'll check ("we look on 1 December, 3 days after the last post").
- **Risks:** 2–3 plain ones with a simple fix, for example: not enough photos (generate backgrounds, or ask for 3 more photos); more posts than they can keep up with (cut the rhythm); an offer that may change (confirm terms and dates before drafting); a claim that needs backing (the brand check will flag it).
- **Next step:** "If the plan looks right, I'll draft all the posts and visuals and show you a summary to approve. Nothing gets scheduled until you say so."

## The short card (show this in chat first)

```
Campaign: <name>
Goal: <goal> · Number to watch: <number and date>
For: <audience in one line>
Core message: <one sentence>
Networks and rhythm: <networks>, <N> posts per week each
Arc: <arc in one line> · <N> posts between <first date> and <last date>
Images: <N> own photos · <N> generated · <N> composed (estimated cost: US$<x> from estimate_cost)
Free slots: <N>
```

Then ask: "Does this plan work for you? I can show the full calendar table if you want to see every post."

## After the campaign

When the last post is out (or the user asks how it went), do the check-in from `reports.md`, and save lessons to the brand card's **Learned** section.
