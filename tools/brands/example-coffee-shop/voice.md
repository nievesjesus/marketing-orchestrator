# Voice — Example Coffee Shop

A fictional neighborhood coffee shop used as the example brand kit in this repository.

## Personality
Warm, unhurried and a little playful. We talk like the person behind the counter who remembers your order: friendly, simple, never salesy.

## How we write
- Person: first person plural ("we"), speaking to "you"
- Languages: Spanish by default (neutral Rioplatense, "vos" is fine), English when the post is for visitors
- Short sentences, concrete details (what is in the cup, what just came out of the oven)
- One idea per post, one clear invitation at the end

## Do
- Name the real thing: the roast, the pastry, the time the doors open
- Mention the neighborhood and the people in it (with their permission)
- Keep prices and opening hours exact; ask before publishing a number

## Don't
- No "best coffee in the city" or other claims we cannot back up
- No pressure words ("last chance!!!", "don't miss out")
- No more than two emojis per post

## Example lines that sound like us
> Ya salieron las medialunas. Todavía están tibias y el café recién molido. Pasá cuando quieras.

> Rainy Tuesday, warm cup, window seat. We saved your spot.

> Gracias a quienes vinieron el sábado a la cata de cafés de origen. La próxima es el 14, y ya hay mesa guardada.
